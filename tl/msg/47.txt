Finally, a brief word on "Reinforce", "Raid" and "Trap".

@47100
"Reinforce" can only be used once an army has been formed with the War command.

@47101
It is used to strengthen the forces bound for a city where a battle is to be fought.

@47102
"Raid" is a command to seize gold and food from a city's people when you are short of them.

@47103
It lowers Farming, Public Safety and so on, so it is best avoided unless you are desperate.

@47104
"Trap" is a command to lay traps in a city in advance, ready for an enemy attack.

@47105
Each kind of trap can only be laid in set places. It works on the square it is laid on and the 8 squares around it.

@47106
If you have gold to spare, it is wise to lay traps often.
